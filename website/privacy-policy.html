Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 31, 2026  ·  Effective: March 31, 2026

Summit Hoops ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Summit Hoops mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when you create an account or use the App:

  • Account information: first name, last name, email address, age, basketball position, and school/team name.
  • Authentication credentials: hashed password; we never store your password in plain text.
  • Subscription and billing data: your selected plan (Training or Elite) and payment processing details. Payments are handled by our third-party payment processor; we do not store full card numbers on our servers.
  • Workout and progress data: completed workouts, workout streaks, XP points, level, badges earned, and performance metrics you log in the App.
  • Video content: videos you voluntarily upload for form checks. These are stored securely and reviewed only by your assigned coach.
  • Messages: communications you send through the in-app coach messaging feature.
  • Device and usage data: device type, operating system version, IP address, app version, and general usage patterns (e.g., screens visited, features used) collected automatically to help us improve the App.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account and authenticate your identity.
  • Deliver personalized workout plans and coaching tailored to your profile.
  • Process subscription payments and manage your billing relationship.
  • Enable coach–student communication and form-check video reviews.
  • Track your progress, award XP, and unlock badges.
  • Send you important account and service notifications (e.g., new workouts, schedule updates).
  • Diagnose technical issues, prevent fraud, and improve App stability and features.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. How We Share Your Information

We share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Your coach: Your profile data, workout history, progress metrics, uploaded videos, and messages are visible to the coach assigned to your account so they can provide coaching.
  • Service providers: We use trusted third-party vendors (e.g., cloud hosting, payment processors, video storage) who process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal compliance: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or the public.
  • Business transfers: If we merge with or are acquired by another company, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you before your data becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users of all ages, including minors who train with a coach. If a user is under 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in their country), a parent or legal guardian must create the account and consent to this Privacy Policy on their behalf. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. If you request account deletion, we will delete or anonymize your data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law or legitimate business necessity (e.g., billing records).

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), hashed password storage, and access controls limiting who can view your data. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable precautions to protect your information.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Delete your account and associated personal data.
  • Export a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Opt out of non-essential communications.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below. We will respond within 30 days. California residents may have additional rights under the CCPA; EU/UK residents have rights under the GDPR — both are covered by the rights listed above.

8. Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated personal data by emailing us at privacy@summithoops.com with the subject line "Delete My Account." We will process your request within 30 days.

9. Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services (including cloud infrastructure and payment processors) that have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review those policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, notify you via email or an in-app notice at least 7 days before the change takes effect. Your continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
