Why This Is Different

Not an app.
A coaching relationship.

Every other training app gives you the same pre-built programs everyone else is running. We do the opposite — your coach studies your game, identifies your specific weaknesses, and builds every single workout around you.

"This isn't a generic drill library or AI-generated plan. Every workout is hand-built by your coach based on your current skill level, goals, weaknesses, and progress."

True 1-on-1 Personalization
Your coach knows your name, your position, your weaknesses, and your goals. Every drill assigned has a reason. No two players get the same plan.
Two-Way Video Coaching
Watch your coach demonstrate every drill. Then record yourself and send it back — your coach watches it and gives specific feedback on your form.
Monthly In-Person Evaluations
The digital program is anchored in real-world accountability. Monthly in-person sessions track your progress and evolve your training phase.
The Process

Up and training in 3 steps

1

Train, Submit, Improve
Open the app, complete your daily workout with coach-recorded drill videos. Submit form checks. Get feedback. Watch yourself improve week over week.
What You Get

Built for serious young players

  • Grades K–12, all skill levels
    Whether you're just picking up a basketball or preparing for varsity tryouts — plans are calibrated to your exact level.
